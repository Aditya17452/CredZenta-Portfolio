The problem

Credit appraisal is still a paper chase.

Most lenders still appraise loans the way they did a decade ago — spreadsheets, scanned PDFs and a dozen portals. It's slow, it's inconsistent, and it doesn't scale.

01{{icon:pen}}

Manual data entry

Analysts spend hours re-keying financial statements and bank data before any real analysis starts.

02{{icon:search}}

Scattered verification

Financials, banking and tax data sit in different places, so cross-checking them is tedious and easy to miss.

03{{icon:file-text}}

Inconsistent memos

Every analyst writes differently, which makes reviews slower and decisions harder to compare.

04{{icon:history}}

Thin audit trails

When a decision is questioned later, the reasoning is often buried in emails and notes.
