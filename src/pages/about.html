About CredZenta

Making credit decisions faster, fairer and easier to trust.

Our story

India's small businesses are the backbone of its economy — yet getting credit is still slow, and assessing it is still largely manual. Lenders want to say yes to good businesses faster, but their teams are buried in documents, spreadsheets and portals.

CredZenta was founded in 2026 to change that. We're building the intelligence layer for credit appraisal: software that does the groundwork in hours, explains every insight, and leaves the final decision with the people accountable for it.
