Technology

Specialist AI, working together.

CredZenta is built on a coordinated, multi-agent AI architecture — where each component does one job exceptionally well, and every output is checked, explained and reviewable.

Architecture

Not one model. A coordinated system.

Instead of relying on a single general-purpose model, CredZenta uses specialised AI components for different parts of the appraisal — reading documents, analysing financials, verifying information and assembling the memo. An orchestration layer coordinates them, and deterministic checks keep the output grounded in your data and your policy.
